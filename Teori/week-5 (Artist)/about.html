THE STORY OF
ONE 'O ONES

Born from late-night basement tracker experiments in Minneapolis, ONE 'O ONES bridges binary digital synthesis (1s and 0s) with visceral human wonder.

Inspired by early computer demoscene trackers, analog tape saturation, and twilight dusk strolls, ONE 'O ONES constructs three distinct sonic universes: retro chiptune gaming nostalgia (8-Bit Odyssey), bold minimalist twilight ambient accompanied by glowing fireflies (Astral Horizon), and deep submerged aquatic dream-pop (Ocean Lullaby).

8-Bit Odyssey

Pure chiptune square waves, arpeggiated melodic counterpoint, and crunchy bitcrushed percussion. Built primarily on modified Game Boy consoles and vintage sound chips.

Astral Horizon

Ocean Lullaby
